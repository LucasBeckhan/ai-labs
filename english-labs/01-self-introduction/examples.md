## 示例 1：初级工程师（1-3 年经验）

### 背景
- **姓名**：Sarah Wang
- **职位**：Software Engineer
- **经验**：2 年
- **公司**：申请 Google 初级后端工程师

### 30 秒版本

```
Hi, I'm Sarah Wang, a Software Engineer with 2 years of experience in backend development. 
I specialize in Python and distributed systems. 
Recently, I built a microservice that handles 10,000 requests per second with 99.9% uptime. 
I'm excited about Google because of your innovative work in cloud infrastructure.
```

### 2 分钟版本

```
Thank you for this opportunity. I'm Sarah Wang, a Software Engineer with 2 years of experience 
in backend development.

I graduated from Zhejiang University with a degree in Computer Science. 
I started my career at Tencent, where I worked on their payment platform, 
handling millions of transactions daily.

Over the past 2 years, I've developed expertise in Python and distributed systems. 
For example, I designed and implemented a microservice for user authentication that handles 
10,000 requests per second with 99.9% uptime. 
I also optimized our database queries, reducing response time by 40%.

I'm now looking to work on larger-scale systems and learn from world-class engineers. 
I believe Google is the perfect place because of your innovative work in cloud infrastructure 
and your engineering culture.

I'm particularly interested in Google Cloud Platform, especially services like Pub/Sub and Dataflow, 
which I've been learning about.

Thank you for your time. I'm excited to discuss how I can contribute to your team.
```

### 关键表达分析

| 表达 | 中文 | 为什么好 |
|------|------|----------|
| "handles 10,000 requests per second" | 处理每秒 1 万请求 | 具体数字，有说服力 |
| "99.9% uptime" | 99.9% 可用性 | 量化成果 |
| "reducing response time by 40%" | 响应时间减少 40% | 展示优化能力 |
| "world-class engineers" | 世界级工程师 | 表达对公司的尊重 |
| "I've been learning about" | 我一直在学习 | 展现主动性 |

## 常见改进点

### 1. 开头太普通
**普通**：My name is Alex. I'm a software engineer.

**改进**：Thank you for this opportunity. I'm Alex, a Senior Software Engineer with 6 years of experience specializing in distributed systems.

### 2. 缺乏具体数字
**普通**：I improved system performance.

**改进**：I improved system throughput by 300% and reduced P99 latency from 500ms to 50ms.

### 3. 没有展现热情
**普通**：I want to work at your company.

**改进**：I'm particularly excited about your work on real-time personalization, which I've been following and find truly innovative.

### 4. 结尾太突兀
**普通**：That's all. Thank you.

**改进**：I'm really looking forward to discussing how my experience can contribute to your team. Thank you for your time.
